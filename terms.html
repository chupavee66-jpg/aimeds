AIMEDS — Terms of Service

Last updated: 27 August 2026  ·  Applies to chupavee66-jpg.github.io/aimeds

Not for clinical use. AIMEDS is a teaching and research tool built around fictional patients. It is not a medical device, not a clinical decision-support system, and not a source of medical guidance. Some clinical content is still under faculty review. Never use anything from AIMEDS — including anything the AI assistant says — to make a decision about a real patient.

Who may use it

AIMEDS is provided to students and staff of Phramongkutklao College of Medicine taking part in the study described in our Privacy Notice. You need a Google account to sign in, so that your attempts can be linked across sessions.

Your account

  • Sign in with your own Google account. Do not use someone else's.
  • On a shared computer, use Sign out in the main menu when you finish — otherwise the next person's work is recorded against your account and it cannot be separated afterwards.
  • Choose a nickname that is appropriate for a shared classroom leaderboard. We may change or remove nicknames that are offensive, or that impersonate another person.

Acceptable use

  • Do not attempt to interfere with the service, its database, or other participants' data.
  • Do not type real patient information, or anyone's personal details, into the AI assistant or any answer box.
  • Do not use the built-in AI assistant for purposes unrelated to the cases. It is provided for learning support and its usage is metered.
  • Case content, questions and feedback are teaching material belonging to their authors. Please do not redistribute them.

Participation and assessment

Taking part is voluntary. Your scores are collected for research and are not used for course grading unless Phramongkutklao College of Medicine tells you otherwise in writing. Declining or withdrawing will not affect your studies.

Availability

AIMEDS is provided as-is, with no guarantee of availability or of data preservation. It depends on third-party services that may be interrupted. Note in particular that if you close the browser tab in the middle of a case, the answers from that case are not saved — use QUIT TO MENU from the pause menu if you need to stop partway.

Liability

To the fullest extent permitted by law, Phramongkutklao College of Medicine and the research team accept no liability for any loss arising from use of AIMEDS, and give no warranty as to the accuracy or completeness of its clinical content.

Changes

These terms may be updated; the date above will change when they are.

บัญชีผู้ใช้

  • เข้าสู่ระบบด้วยบัญชี Google ของตนเองเท่านั้น
  • หากใช้เครื่องคอมพิวเตอร์ร่วมกับผู้อื่น ให้กด ออกจากระบบ เมื่อเล่นเสร็จ มิฉะนั้นผลการเล่นของคนถัดไปจะถูกบันทึกในบัญชีของคุณ และไม่สามารถแยกออกได้ภายหลัง
  • ตั้งชื่อเล่นให้เหมาะสมกับกระดานคะแนนที่ทุกคนมองเห็น เราขอสงวนสิทธิ์แก้ไขหรือลบชื่อที่ไม่เหมาะสม หรือแอบอ้างเป็นบุคคลอื่น

ข้อปฏิบัติ

  • ห้ามรบกวนหรือแทรกแซงระบบ ฐานข้อมูล หรือข้อมูลของผู้เข้าร่วมคนอื่น
